How Tabs Pin works

Save the sites you always keep open. Reopen them in one click, without duplicates.

  1. 📌

    1 Save a page

    Open the Tabs Pin popup and click Pin Current Tab.

  2. 🚀

    2 Reopen them anytime

    One click brings them back as pinned tabs, even after a browser restart.

  3. 🗂️

    3 Keep them apart

    A category per project, each with its own icon and its own tabs.

Everything stays in your browser: no account, no server, no tracking.
