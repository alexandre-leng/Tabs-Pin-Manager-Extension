How Tabs Pin works

Save the sites you always keep open, then reopen them as pinned tabs in one click, without duplicates.

  1. 1

    Pin a site

    Click the Tabs Pin icon, then "Pin Current Tab" to save the page you are on. Add the others from the options page.

  2. 2

    Reopen them later

    Open a category to restore its sites, or "Open all tabs" for everything, once, even after closing your browser.

  3. 3

    Sort them by project

    Each category has its own icon and its own tabs, so work, personal and reading lists stay apart.

Everything stays in your browser: no account, no server, no tracking.
